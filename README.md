# Swippy 🍔🎥
## Reels + Zomato

### Users -> can see food reels, view the food items
### Food Partners -> can list food items

Swippy is a food discovery platform that combines short-form video content (like reels) with food exploration.
Users can scroll through engaging food videos, discover dishes, and explore items from different food partners in a fun and interactive way.

The goal of Swippy is to make food discovery more visual, immersive, and entertaining.
